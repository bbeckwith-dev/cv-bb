CLI tool that transcribes audio recordings and processes text documents into structured, categorized markdown for Obsidian vaults. ### CCsimulator
**github.com/bbeckwith-dev/CCsimulator** | JavaScript, HTML/CSS, Chart.js

Browser-based covered call options simulator for learning option mechanics. Implements Black-Scholes European call pricing with all five Greeks, geometric Brownian motion for price simulation, real-time risk gauges with zone-based alerts, payoff diagrams, and a rolling simulator. Includes 7 preset market scenarios (IV crush, BTC pump, whipsaw, etc.) and a learning mode with inline explanations. Single-file vanilla JS (~1,700 lines), no build step.
